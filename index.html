Your hands are tracked with MediaPipe entirely in this browser. Video is never uploaded or recorded.

Your browser will ask for camera permission next. Keep your hand 40–70 cm from the camera, palm facing it.

Welcome to the Courtyard House

A single-storey house around an open courtyard, built at real size (1:1). Walk in through the front door.

  • Thumb + Index walk forward where you look
  • Thumb + Ring turn left
  • Thumb + Pinky turn right
  • Let go stop immediately

Interactive: doors open as you approach · touch (VR) or click (desktop) the glowing wall buttons to switch room lights · touch the orb in the courtyard to switch day / night.

Quest 3: open this page in Meta Quest Browser, press Enter VR, then put the controllers down.
Desktop: press Start Camera, or use W A S D and drag to look.
